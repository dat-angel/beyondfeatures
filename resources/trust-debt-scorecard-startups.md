## What this is

Early-stage teams often optimize for **tech debt** (velocity, shipping) and under-invest in **trust debt** (signals buyers, investors, and procurement use *before* they ever get to your roadmap).

Trust debt has two dimensions:

| Dimension | Owner lens | Question it answers |
|-----------|------------|---------------------|
| **Security trust** | Product, eng, security | Can we keep our word on safety, compliance, reliability, and how we handle data? |
| **Market trust** | PMM, founder narrative, sales | Do buyers *believe* we do what we say — especially under scrutiny? |

**Tech debt** usually costs **velocity**. **Trust debt** often costs **revenue, fundraising, and shortlist inclusion** before you know you were in the running.

## How to score

For each row, pick **one** score:

| Score | Meaning |
|:-----:|---------|
| **0** | No — missing or not credible |
| **1** | In progress — exists but thin, stale, undocumented, or inconsistent |
| **2** | Yes — current, documented, and easy for a buyer to verify |

**Security Trust** subtotal: **\_\_\_ / 10** (five rows × max 2)  
**Market Trust** subtotal: **\_\_\_ / 10** (five rows × max 2)  
**Total: \_\_\_ / 20**

Score **honestly for internal planning**. This is a prioritization tool, not a certification.

---

## Security trust (Amber’s section) — /10

| # | Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|--------|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Trust page** — Can a buyer find a **security / trust overview** on your site in under 60 seconds? | No trust page | Exists but stale or thin | Current, linked from homepage or footer, includes status + compliance entry points |
| **2** | **Compliance path** — SOC 2 (or comparable) **status**? | No path | In progress (vendor engaged, scope defined, etc.) | Type I or Type II **attested in the last 12 months** (or equivalent your buyers accept) |
| **3** | **Questionnaire readiness** — Can you respond to a **SIG / CAIQ** (or your category equivalent) within **5 business days**? | No template; scrambling | Have answers but ad hoc | Maintained template + **named owner** |
| **4** | **Transparency artifacts** — **Subprocessors**, **DPA**, **incident response policy** public (where applicable)? | None public | Some; mostly “available on request” | Public, current, **version-controlled** or clearly dated |
| **5** | **Third-party validation** — Recent **pen test** (or equivalent) with **shareable summary**? | Never tested | Tested; no shareable artifact | Within **12 months**; summary shareable under NDA (or better) |

**Security Trust subtotal: \_\_\_ / 10**

## Market trust (Beatriz’s section) — /10

| # | Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|--------|---|---|---|
| **1** | **One-sentence test** — Can a **stranger** explain what you do after **30 seconds** on your homepage? | No | Sort of | Yes — consistently, without jargon drift |
| **2** | **Founder narrative alignment** — Does the founder’s **public voice** (LinkedIn, podcasts, etc.) match what the company sells? | Disconnect | Loosely aligned | Tight, mutually reinforcing |
| **3** | **Reference customers** — **3+** customers willing to take a **reference call** within **30 days**? | 0–1 | 2 | **3+**, recent, willing |
| **4** | **Case study quality** — Dated, specific, **measurable**? | Generic or none | Some; vague metrics | Dated, **named**, with **metrics** |
| **5** | **Demo–deck–product alignment** — Does the **sales deck** match a **live demo**? | Significant gap | Some “smoothing” in deck | **Demo-true** deck |

**Market Trust subtotal: \_\_\_ / 10**

## Total and interpretation

**Total: \_\_\_ / 20**

| Range | Read |
|------:|------|
| **0–7** | Trust debt is likely **blocking revenue and fundraising**. Pause broad GTM spend; close the highest-risk gaps first. |
| **8–13** | **Material gaps.** Buyers feel friction; diligence gets harder. Aim to close top gaps **this quarter**. |
| **14–17** | **Foundation is in place.** Shift from “existence” to **signal density** and maintenance. |
| **18–20** | Trust posture is a **competitive moat** — protect it with cadence (reviews, freshness, incident muscle). |

## After you score (5 minutes)

1. What is the **highest-leverage** gap on the scorecard?  
2. Which **30-day** action are you committing to **before Friday**?  
3. Where do you need **help** (vendor, intro, template, reviewer)?

Use the **[90-day playbook](trust-debt-90-day-playbook.md)** to sequence work without boiling the ocean.

## Facilitating in 60 minutes

Use this when the room has **one clock** for intro + panel + scoring + Q&A (networking happens **after**, in the hall or at dinner).

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **0–10m** | Hook: trust debt vs tech debt; two dimensions (Beatriz + Amber, tight). |
| **10–20m** | Mini-panel — **at most four** moderator questions; no career arcs. |
| **20–22m** | Transition: open scorecard (print or QR), explain 0/1/2 once. |
| **22–34m** | **Silent self-score** (security + market). |
| **34–42m** | **Pairs** — one surprise + one highest-leverage gap each. |
| **42–47m** | **Plenary** — show of hands by band (0–7 / 8–13 / 14+) **or** two cold-called examples; keep it diagnostic, not performative. |
| **47–57m** | Q&A. |
| **57–60m** | Close: **one** 30-day commitment each; point to playbook **Days 1–30** only (do not read the full 90 days aloud). |

**Pre-work (optional):** Ask attendees to skim the scorecard **before** the session so live time goes to **interpretation and commitments**, not first read.
