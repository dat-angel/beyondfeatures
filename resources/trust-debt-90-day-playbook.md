## What this is

A **sequenced** plan to pay down **trust debt** across **security trust** and **market trust** in roughly **90 days**. Pair it with the [scorecard](trust-debt-scorecard-startups.md): score first, then pick **one** must-ship thread per dimension so the work compounding.

**Principle:** Tech debt is often refactorable. Trust debt has **long memory** — buyers, agents, and diligence processes remember gaps. Ship **credible artifacts** (pages, templates, dated proof) over **volume**.

**On a 60-minute stage block:** Do **not** walk through all three phases live. Assign **Days 1–30** as homework and use Q&A for sequencing questions. Facilitator timing lives with the [scorecard](trust-debt-scorecard-startups.md#facilitating-in-60-minutes) (60-minute section).

---

## Days 1–30: Assess + close credibility gaps

### Security trust

- Stand up a **trust page** (trust center starter) — use a GRC automation vendor (**Vanta**, **Drata**, etc.) or a **hand-rolled** one-pager if you must, but make it **findable** and **dated**.
- Build **one** questionnaire response template — start with **SIG Lite** or **CAIQ** skeleton; assign an **owner**.
- Document **subprocessors** and **DPA** posture; publish what you can **without** waiting for perfection.

### Market trust

- **Homepage audit** for the **one-sentence** clarity test; rewrite until a stranger passes it.
- Founder writes a **~200-word “why now”** narrative; **pin** to LinkedIn (or your primary founder surface).
- **Case study inventory** — archive generic ones; list gaps you will fill in days 31–60.

**Exit criteria (30):** A buyer can find **who you are**, **what you do**, and **how you handle trust/security** without a scavenger hunt.

---

## Days 31–60: Build proof points

### Security trust

- Schedule **pen test** or formal **compliance kickoff** (if not already in flight).
- Stand up a real **status page** with history (not a marketing uptime claim).
- Implement a **questionnaire SLA**: respond within **5 business days** (calendar it).

### Market trust

- Land **3 reference customers** with **written** approval to take calls.
- Ship **2 new case studies** — **dated**, **named**, **metrics**.
- **Founder–product–deck alignment audit** — sales deck matches live demo; fix drift.

**Exit criteria (60):** You can **prove** claims with **artifacts** and **people** (references), not adjectives.

---

## Days 61–90: Codify + signal

### Security trust

- Publish a **trust center** bundle: subprocessors, DPA, incident response, compliance **status** (as appropriate).
- Publish a **security architecture overview** for the **technical buyer** (diagram + data flows + boundaries).
- Run an **internal incident drill**; **document** outcomes and follow-ups (redacts sensitive details).

### Market trust

- **Sales-to-demo audit** — close remaining deck/demo gaps; version the deck.
- Founder publishes **one substantive POV post** (minimum) this month — thesis, not vibes.
- Set a **monthly trust-debt review** (recurring calendar) — scorecard refresh + owner for drift (docs, claims, trust page).

**Exit criteria (90):** Trust work is **on a cadence**, not a one-off project; new marketing does not silently re-open debt.

---

## Working session debrief (5 minutes)

Use after a team offsite or founder working block:

1. What is the **highest-leverage** gap on the scorecard **right now**?  
2. What **30-day** action are you committing to **before Friday**?  
3. Where do you need **outside help** (tooling, intro, template, reviewer)?
