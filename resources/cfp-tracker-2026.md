# CFP Tracker (2026–2027)

**By [Beatriz Datangel Rodgers](https://linkedin.com/in/bzdata)** — [Beyond Features](https://www.beyondfeatures.xyz)

**Updated:** August 13, 2026

A working list of security, AI, and developer conference CFPs — plus events that do **not** take public submissions, so you do not chase a form that does not exist.

**Topic tags:** `[Security]` `[AI]` `[Dev]` `[GTM/Ent]` (GTM / entrepreneurship)

**Fit:** `Tier 1` = strong AppSec / security speaking fit · `Tier 2` = solid developer or security fit · `Tier 3` = curated, invite-only, hackathon, or GTM-led

Confirm dates and links on the organizer site before you submit. Windows shift.
